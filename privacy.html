Your space. Your data.

Private-preview data notice · updated 18 October 2026. This describes the current implementation. Public-launch policies and the operator’s applicable legal obligations still need final review.

What Minimal stores

Your tasks, notes and unfinished drafts, calendar events, food and water logs, training, focus history, habits, preferences and optional planning profile save to your account on the hosted version. Some records may reveal personal health information. Enter only information you want this service to store.

Where it goes

The current website uses Sites sign-in and a Cloudflare D1 account database. The server uses the hosting platform’s verified account identity to choose your records. Minimal does not store your sign-in password in browser storage. It does not upload your records to the GitHub code repository.

Minimal has no advertising or analytics SDK installed. The hosting and sign-in providers may process technical logs under their own policies. Their exact retention, region and operational backup settings have not been independently verified for this project.

Copies on your device

Hosted accounts use a temporary tab copy by default. Browsers may restore that copy after reopening a session. Remember this device is optional and stores an unencrypted copy. Browser access, extensions and shared profiles can expose these copies. Sign out and clear site data on shared devices. Practice and device-only previews save locally and do not provide account sync.

Nutrition lookups and other requests

USDA references and bundled food/exercise data are served with the app. Barcode lookup sends the barcode and normal browser connection information to Open Food Facts, not your meal history. The developer avatar can load from GitHub. Opening source links connects to those providers. Planning suggestions are computed on your device. No meal-photo recognition or wearable connection is active.

Saving, archives and backups

Account saving happens automatically. Online food/water archives remain part of your account and are not independent backups. Optional full-backup downloads are encrypted in your browser with your chosen passphrase. The passphrase is not sent to Minimal and cannot be recovered. The old active-record JSON export and individual archive downloads are unencrypted. Protect downloaded files yourself.

Access, correction and deletion

Read/edit current records in the app. Read older food/water logs in Settings → View online archives. Export active records or a full encrypted backup in Settings. Deleting Minimal data removes the current/previous account payload and online archives from the app’s database, but does not delete your OpenAI account, downloaded files, other browsers’ copies, or any provider-managed recovery/log copies. Rate-limit counters and an account identifier can remain for abuse prevention. Clear this device removes browser copies separately.

Previous active saves rotate when records change. Online archives remain until account-data deletion or an explicit full-backup restore replaces them. Automatic off-site service backups are not configured. Provider recovery retention must be confirmed before public launch; no instant erasure from every underlying backup is promised.

Who it is for

Minimal is intended for adults organizing everyday routines. It does not diagnose conditions, provide medical treatment, or guarantee the exact nutrients in a meal. Suitability for children and requirements for each launch region remain part of the public-release review.
