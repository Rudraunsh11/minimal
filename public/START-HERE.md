# Your first five minutes with Minimal

Open [your app](https://minimal-rudraunsh-life.pearlyplum8.chatgpt.site/) and refresh once to load the new version. The same OpenAI account that owns the private site is required.

## 1. Start with the practice demo

Click **Practice demo** on Today, or find it in Settings. The yellow banner confirms that you are using sample records. Your real records stay in a different storage area. **Reset demo** resets only sample records; **Return to my app** brings back your real records.

Click **How to use Minimal** at the top for an eight-step guided tour. Use Next and Back, or Try this screen to close the tour and explore. You can reopen it anytime.

## Planner: tasks, notes and calendar together

On a phone, tap **Planner** in the bottom bar. The three labelled shortcuts jump to separate Tasks, Notes and Calendar sections. Use **Add task**, **Add note** or **Add event** in the matching section. Tasks and events follow the selected date; notes show your latest saved ideas across all days. These are your existing records, not duplicate copies.

## 2. Log a food with Quick Add

Open **Food**, then choose **+ Quick add food**. Check the date and meal first. The Home page has no global Quick Add. Tasks, Notes and Calendar each have their own add action.

### When you know the food but not its nutrition

1. Type `rice noodles cooked` in the search box.
2. Choose the USDA SR Legacy record named `Rice noodles, cooked` (108 kcal per 100 g).
3. Enter `150` in Amount eaten (g).
4. Check the preview: 162 kcal, calculated as 108 × 150 ÷ 100.
5. Press Save. Nutrition now shows the entry in your selected meal and updates the daily totals.

Use the actual weight of your food; raw and cooked food records differ. A household portion uses a reference weight and may differ from your actual portion.

### When you already know the calories or macros

1. Open Quick Add → **Enter calories / macros**.
2. Enter a name such as `Protein bar`.
3. Enter the totals for what you ate: for example, 220 kcal and 20 g protein.
4. Leave carbohydrates and fat blank if you do not know them.
5. Press **Log food**.

These figures describe the complete entry, not 100 g. Blank values stay unknown. A food name alone does not generate an estimate. You must enter at least calories or one macro. In Nutrition, use Edit to correct a Quick Add entry.

### When you eat the same thing again

Open Quick Add and choose **Add again** beside a recent food. This repeats the same amount and nutrients for the chosen date and meal. If you ate a different amount, use Portion on the new meal entry.

For packaged food, **Use a barcode** accepts the printed barcode digits. Verify the returned product and whether its label is per 100 g or 100 ml. Camera scanning is not included. **Read a label** saves a custom reference food: enter the label's basis amount, such as 30 g, and its printed nutrients. Then select that food to log your eaten portion.

## 3. Read your daily nutrition

Calories and nutrients update automatically from the meal entries. A dash means unknown. `2/3 foods have data · partial` means the sum covers only two of the three entries. It is not your full intake for that nutrient.

Use Food library & recipes to create recipes. Add each ingredient's weighed amount, then enter the final edible cooked weight. This accounts for yield, but does not model nutrient losses from cooking or discarded liquid. Missing ingredient nutrients remain unknown in the recipe.

Nutrition goals are optional targets you enter yourself, not calculated dietary advice.

## 4. Plan and track your day

- **Tasks:** add a priority. Put checklist steps on separate lines. Tick each step and mark the parent task complete when finished.
- **Calendar:** click a date and add an event with start and end times. Reminders appear only while the app is open and browser timers run.
- **Water:** use +250 ml or +500 ml on Today or Nutrition. Undo last reverses the latest drink for the selected day.
- **Habits:** add a simple daily habit and tick it on days you do it.
- **Movement:** log exercise name, sets, repetitions per set, and added weight. For example, three sets of eight bodyweight squats use 0 kg added weight. Log separate entries if weights or reps differ between sets.
- **Notes:** add a title and text, then Save. Search finds both title and body. Notes do not autosave in this version.

## 5. Keep your data safe

Settings → Export backup downloads your records as a JSON file. Keep it somewhere private. Restore replaces the current records only after confirmation.

Data belongs to the current browser and website address. Moving from a local file to the online site does not transfer it automatically: export at the old address, then restore at the new one. OpenAI sign-in grants access to the private site; it does not sync your logs across devices.

Hints are shown initially. Use **Hide hints** on Today or in Settings once you are comfortable. You can turn them back on in Settings. Essential data limitations stay visible even when hints are hidden.

## Rewards and Training

Open **Rewards** to see your streak, level, monthly goals and badges. Completing a task, checking a habit, logging learning, recording steps or finishing training can keep a streak. Tag school or college tasks **Academic** to count them toward the academic challenge. Use **Log learning** for a topic and minutes; **Log steps** replaces the chosen day's total with the total shown on your device.

Open **Training → Start workout**, name your session, then **Add exercise**. Set weights and reps independently for each row and tick each completed set. These edits save on this browser. You can navigate away or refresh and resume. **Finish workout → Save completed workout** adds only checked sets to history. From history, choose **Save as routine**; start it next time with all completion checkboxes reset. Rest timers work while this page is open and reset on refresh.

Unfinished note drafts now save on this device while you type. Reopen the same note (or New note for a new draft) to recover it. Press Save to add it to your collection. Recipe ingredients now have a search field, and vitamins/minerals can be expanded on the nutrition screen.
