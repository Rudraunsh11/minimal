A little help.

Minimal is built by Rudraunsh. For a bug, describe which page you used, the action you tried, what happened, and your browser/device. Include a screenshot only after removing personal information.

Contact the developer · GitHub profile

Never email passwords, sign-in codes, backup passphrases, food/health logs or full backup files. Support does not need your credentials.

Did my changes save?

Look for “Saved to your account.” If saving is unavailable, keep the tab open, reconnect and choose Check sync. Temporary tab copies may be lost when closed. If two versions conflict, review them and safeguard both before replacing either.

Do I need to export every day?

No. Account saving is automatic. An optional full encrypted backup is an extra copy for your own safekeeping. Store it off this device with its passphrase kept separately.

Where is my older history?

Logs you explicitly archived are in Settings → View online archives. Archives are read-only and do not appear in the daily diary. The current version supports ten food/water archives.

Food numbers and training

Match the food’s preparation, portion weight and product market. Homemade dishes are estimates. Unknown nutrients are not zero. Training logs do not verify movement technique or connect to a wearable.

App installation

You can use the responsive website in your phone’s browser. Native App Store/Google Play packages have not been released. The install manifest does not provide offline account access or closed-app notifications.
